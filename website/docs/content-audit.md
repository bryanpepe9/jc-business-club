## Privacy policy (`/privacidade`) — draft

Describes only what the site actually does: the form fields collected, Cloudflare
(hosting, Turnstile), Resend (e-mail delivery), no advertising or tracking cookies,
and the data-subject rights in LGPD art. 18.

**⚑ Legal review** of the whole text, in particular: the legal bases cited
(art. 7º I, V, IX), the international-transfer paragraph, the retention wording,
and the commitments "Não usamos seus dados para publicidade e não os vendemos."
The data controller's legal name, CNPJ and contact are missing; the policy must
not launch without them.

## Claims deliberately not made

None of these appear anywhere on the site. The content model has optional
fields for them, so they can be added once confirmed:

- Minimum commitment (e.g. six months), cancellation, refund, renewal or payment terms
- Remote or international participation; any geographic wording ("São Paulo · Brasil · Internacional")
- 1:1 meetings with Jennifer
- Member capacity or a numeric limit
- Breakfast venue, dates or schedule
- Commission or success-fee arrangements
- Founder biography, JC Capital role, headshot
- Press mentions (hidden until each article has a verified URL)
- Testimonials, member counts, statistics, partner logos, deal outcomes — none exist and none were invented
