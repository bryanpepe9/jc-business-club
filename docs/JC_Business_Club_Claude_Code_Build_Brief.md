## Positioning and visual direction

Create a premium, modern, sophisticated, international-quality business brand.

Visual direction:
- Minimal, elegant, editorial, and corporate.
- Palette: black, graphite, charcoal, gray, white, with very restrained metallic accents if appropriate.
- Avoid excessive gold, glossy effects, gradients that look flashy, luxury clichés, and anything resembling a nightclub or social club.
- Modern, clean, confident typography with strong hierarchy.
- High-quality photography: executive conversations, business leaders, contemporary architecture, São Paulo business environments, and authentic JC Club gatherings.
- Prioritize authentic supplied photographs over generic stock. Never imply stock-photo people are actual members or the founder.
- Use generous whitespace, excellent alignment, subtle motion, and refined transitions.
- Fast loading, responsive, accessible, and polished on mobile and desktop.

The website should feel selective and credible, not boastful or overpromising.
